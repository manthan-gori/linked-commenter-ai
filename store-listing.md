## Detailed Description
Linked Commenter AI helps you write engaging, authentic LinkedIn comments in seconds using cutting-edge AI models (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude).

**✨ Key Features**
- **Inline Assistant**: Clean `✨ AI Comment` button added directly above LinkedIn comment fields.
- **18 Professional Tones + Custom Tones**:
  - Daily: 🎉 Congratulations, 👍 Positive Feedback, 🙏 Appreciation, ✅ Agreement, 💪 Encouragement
  - Growth: 💡 Valuable Notes, 🔥 Engagement, 😂 Funny Viral
  - Classic: 💼 Professional, 🧠 Insightful, 😊 Friendly, ☕ Casual, 😄 Witty, ❤️ Empathetic, 🤔 Curious, 🚀 Motivational, ⚡ Contrarian, 📖 Storytelling
- **Multi-Model Support**: Connect Groq (Compound, Qwen), Google Gemini (3.7 Flash, 3.5), OpenAI (GPT-4o, o3-mini), or Anthropic (Claude 3.7/3.5).
- **Custom Persona Prompting**: Define your background and role so the AI writes with your genuine voice.
- **Humanized Output**: Automatically removes em-dashes (`—`), hashtags, corporate buzzwords, and reasoning tags.
- **100% Private & Client-Side**: Uses your own direct API keys. Zero tracking, zero telemetry, no external servers.
