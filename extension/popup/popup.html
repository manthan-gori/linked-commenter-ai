Linked Commenter AI

LinkedIn Only Extension
This extension operates on LinkedIn. Open linkedin.com to use AI comment generation.
AI Provider & Model Not configured
How to Use
  1. Open linkedin.com in Google Chrome.
  2. Click into the Comment Box of any post.
  3. Click the AI Comment button above the box.
  4. Review the AI text and click Post!
